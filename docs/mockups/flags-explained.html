What this does

When someone asks for a guide but never opens our DM, we leave a public comment on the reel telling them to check their message requests. We are testing how long to wait before doing that.

Who it affects: people who commented a keyword and were sent a guide, but have not opened the message. Roughly 190 people a week.

What this does

Writes a suggested reply for each conversation waiting on you, so you can send it with one tap instead of typing from scratch.

Who it affects: only you. Nothing is sent to anyone — a draft sits in the Conversations screen until you approve it.

If you turn it off: the suggestions stop appearing. Nothing already sent is affected, and you can still reply by hand.
