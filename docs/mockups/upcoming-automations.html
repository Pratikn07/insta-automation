Below the grid, not above it. The grid is what the page is for. She scans it, doesn’t find the post she just scheduled, and keeps going — this is where that scan ends. A failure doesn’t need to be high on this page, because Home catches it (proposal 2).

A row like CARDBOARD is a receipt, not an entry: once attached it is an ordinary keyword and already shows on its tile in the grid above, so it drops out 24 hours after bound_at rather than living here forever. Waiting and needs-attention rows never expire. The asymmetry is deliberate — a success she misses costs her nothing; a failure she misses costs her every commenter on that post.
