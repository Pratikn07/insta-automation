Signal

The Standing Brief. A daily brief, not a dashboard: the page opens with the finding and puts the numbers underneath as support. Every ratio below is measured against the ground, not chosen by eye.

Colour

One accent for focus and affirmative state, one attention tone that marks a task rather than a failure, and a data vocabulary kept entirely separate from both.

Depth, shape and motion

In-flow content has no shadows. Depth is the paper ground, one barely-raised surface for controls, and rules at two weights. The single exception is a true overlay — the drawer and the confirm dialog float above a scrim and may look like it.

Guardrails

Do

  • Separate with a rule before reaching for a container.
  • Deliver findings on neutral ground.
  • Name the window every clause measured.
  • Keep moss for focus and affirmative state only.
  • Carry counts alongside every rate.
  • Give loading, failed and empty three treatments.
  • Derive every sentence from a measurement.
  • 44px targets and press feedback on coarse pointers.
  • Honour prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Load the typeface, and check the built output.
  • Re-measure every colour if the ground changes.

Don’t

  • Draw a container to create hierarchy.
  • Shadow anything that is not a true overlay.
  • Introduce dark chrome, least of all the rail.
  • Tint a surface that carries a finding.
  • Use moss or a country colour as a general accent.
  • State a percentage without a usable baseline.
  • Show a green status beside a value you do not have.
  • Add a second typeface, or pass weight 500.
  • Write an eyebrow above a heading.
  • Rely on hover for anything a phone user needs.
